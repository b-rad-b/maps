Elevation profile (below the map): the whole trip, day by day, in feet above sea level. Hover or tap it to find that spot on the map. Climb and descent come from a 30 m elevation model, so expect ±10–15%. Highlight and bailout positions were placed by trail mile, so they are approximate.

Sources: Route and stages: Arizona Trail Association GIS (AZT line, passages, trailheads, Harshaw Road connector and Patagonia Train Track Trail, accessed Nov 2026), split into days by Pensador Solutions. Elevation, contours and shaded relief: AWS Terrain Tiles (Copernicus GLO-30 / SRTM); contours generalized from a ~60 m grid, so use them for shape, not navigation. Public land: Esri USA Federal Lands. Highways and towns: US Census TIGER/Line 2024. Border: Natural Earth 1:10m. Locator: US Census cartographic boundary 2023.
Map: Pensador Solutions, November 23, 2026. Web Mercator (EPSG:3857); distances measured on the WGS84 ellipsoid. Built with MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3). Not for navigation: carry the GPX and a real map.
