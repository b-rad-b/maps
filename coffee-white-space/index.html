Market (color)

White space: no Dutch Bros and at most one other drive-thru coffee chain within 2 miles. Saturated: a Dutch Bros, or 4+ drive-thru coffee chains, within 2 miles.

Score (height)

lowhigh

0–100 = demand × openness. Demand ranks people within 2 mi (55%), arterial road (20%), places (15%) and buildings (10%) in the hex. Each other drive-thru coffee within 2 mi cuts openness a little; a Dutch Bros cuts it four times as much.
